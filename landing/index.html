What it does

Lyrics that keep up with the song

No searching, no pasting. Capson notices what's playing and shows the words while you work.

Finds the song for you

When a track starts, Capson reads what your player is showing and looks up the lyrics. You don't type a thing.

Lights up word by word

Each word brightens as the line plays — using the song's own word timing when the lyrics include it.

Make it yours

Seven looks, plus your own font, size, colors and opacity. Drag it anywhere, or hide it with a hotkey.

Make it fit

Seven looks, same words

Pick a style and Capson repaints the lyric. It cycles on its own — tap one to try it. Five caption typographies — Classic, Instagram Story, Big & Small, Spotlight, Karaoke Glow — layer on top of any of these looks.

How it works

Three small steps

1

Play a song

Start your music in the player you already use — a desktop app or a browser tab.

2

Open Capson

Run the single portable file. The overlay appears and starts listening for the song.

3

Let it follow

Lyrics sync to the track. Move it, restyle it, or hide it whenever you like.

Download Capson
